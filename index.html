Short clips from long videos, posted where they belong

takefit is a video editing tool. It takes a long talking-head video or a stream, finds the moments worth sharing, and turns them into finished vertical clips: cut tightly, framed on the speaker, with captions and a title. You review every clip on your phone, approve the ones you like, and post them to YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

How it works

  1. Edit. takefit transcribes the video, picks candidate clips, reframes them to 9:16 and adds captions.
  2. Review. You watch each clip on a review page and tap Approve or Reject. Nothing is posted without your approval.
  3. Post. For TikTok you connect your account once. Then, for each approved clip, you see a preview, edit the caption, choose who can see it and which interactions to allow, declare commercial content if any, and press Post (or send it to your TikTok inbox as a draft to finish in the TikTok app).

TikTok

takefit uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It asks for three permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, to show which account you are posting to.
  • video.upload: to send a clip to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  • video.publish: to post a clip directly to your profile, with the settings you choose.

Your data

takefit only uses your TikTok login to post the clips you choose. It never posts without your action, never adds watermarks or ads to your videos, and you can disconnect it at any time. Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
