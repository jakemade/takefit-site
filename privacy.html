Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What takefit does

takefit edits videos into short clips and posts the clips you approve to YouTube (through YouTube API Services) and TikTok (through TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API).

2. What takefit reads from TikTok

  • Your TikTok display name, avatar and account id (permission user.info.basic), to show which account you are posting to.
  • Your posting options at the moment you post (who can see your posts, whether comments, Duets and Stitches are allowed, the longest video you may post), so the post page only offers choices TikTok allows.
  • The status of each post takefit sends, and its public link once TikTok publishes it.

3. What takefit sends to TikTok

Only the video you chose, with the caption and settings you confirmed (permissions video.upload for drafts and video.publish for direct posts). Nothing is sent before you press Post or Send.

4. Storage

The login tokens TikTok and Google issue are stored in private storage that only takefit's owner can access. They are never shown on this website, never shared, and used only to post the clips you choose. takefit keeps a log of what was posted, when, and its link. takefit does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

5. Your choices

Disconnect takefit from TikTok at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), and from Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After you disconnect, the saved tokens stop working; ask us and we delete them and the posting log.

6. Other policies

Using these features means you also follow the TikTok Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
